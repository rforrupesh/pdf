How to split PDF files

Splitting a PDF lets you pull out specific pages for sharing, trim a long document, or chop a large scan into equal-sized chunks. This tool does all of it inside your browser — no file ever leaves your device.

A few tips

01Page numbers here match the PDF's logical order, not any printed numbers inside the document.
02Use Groups mode to split a long scan into equally-sized chunks — handy for email attachments.
03Marking pages as Deleted removes them from every output mode.
04Rotate individual pages before splitting — the exported PDF will have the right orientation.
