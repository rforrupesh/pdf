How to merge PDF files

Combining PDF documents is something most people need at some point — joining a report with appendices, compiling scanned pages, or packaging multiple files into one clean download. This tool runs entirely in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

A few tips

01 Order your files before merging — drag rows up or down. Fixing the order after means re-merging.
02 Watch the final file size if you're emailing it — Gmail and Outlook cap attachments around 25 MB.
03 This tool doesn't touch your originals. The merge creates a brand-new file and downloads it.
04 Password-protected PDFs won't merge. Unlock them first using the Unlock PDF tool in the nav.
