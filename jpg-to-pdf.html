How to convert JPG to PNG

Converting JPG images to PNG helps preserve image quality and supports transparent backgrounds. This tool works directly in your browser, allowing fast and secure JPG to PNG conversion without uploading your files to any server.

Benefits of PNG images

01 PNG files use lossless compression, helping maintain sharp image quality after conversion.
02 PNG supports transparent backgrounds, making it ideal for logos, icons, and graphic design.
03 PNG images work well for websites, UI elements, screenshots, and digital artwork.
04 Optimized PNG images can improve website appearance while maintaining better visual clarity.

JPG vs PNG

JPG files are smaller and better for photographs, while PNG files are preferred for graphics, transparent backgrounds, and high-quality editing. Choosing the right format depends on your specific image needs and website performance goals.
